# Fictional company example

All company names, figures and source references below are invented to demonstrate the template.

| Metric | Company | Value | Period | Source | Type |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Example A | USD 120 million | FY2025 | Fictional FY2025 report, p. 2 | Reported |
| Revenue | Example A | USD 100 million | FY2024 | Fictional FY2025 report, p. 2 | Reported |
| Revenue growth | Example A | 20% | FY2025 vs. FY2024 | Rows above | Calculated |

**Calculation.** (120 − 100) ÷ 100 = 20%.

**Interpretation.** The example shows higher revenue. The evidence table gives no information about why revenue increased or whether growth continued.

**Review question.** Does the source explain any material contribution from acquisitions, foreign exchange or changes in reporting scope?
